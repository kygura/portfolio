I am not superstitious. I believe in nothing, the material world included. Those who reject God or spirits usually keep matter as a last certainty. I don't keep that one either.

The Pyrrhonists held the same position. They suspended judgment on everything, including the existence of an external world. Sextus Empiricus still ate when hungry and avoided holes in the road. He lived by appearances, acting on how things seem without affirming how they are. Practical life is unchanged; only the metaphysical commitment is gone.

Speculation runs on the same principle. A price is collective belief. Without a belief of your own, you can read everyone else's without being bound by any of them.

## The spellbook

AI is the closest thing to black magic I have seen.

Magic is action on appearances without access to mechanism. That is how everyone works with language models, their builders included. Interpretability research tries to read the spell after it has been cast.

Grimoires demanded exact wording, the correct names in the correct order, because a wrong phrase summoned the wrong thing. Prompting demands the same. A skill file is a written spell: instructions in plain language that make an entity act for you, slightly differently each time.

Every magical tradition held that words act directly on the world. For most of history this was superstition. Today a sentence ships code, sends email and moves money, whether or not I believe in it.

## Pending facts

Quantum mechanics is the closest we have come to understanding the nature of reality. It implies that uncertainty belongs to reality itself, beyond any limit of human knowledge.

Einstein expected the randomness to be ignorance, hidden variables not yet found. In 1964 Bell proved that such variables would have to be nonlocal. Experiments by Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger, awarded the Nobel in 2022, confirmed it.

Nonlocality means the correlations between entangled particles cannot be explained by a signal at or below light speed, or by instructions shared at the start. Measure one particle in Málaga and its partner in Tokyo responds instantly. The link carries no information. Each side alone is noise, and the correlation appears only when both records are compared through ordinary channels, so relativity holds.

Entanglement is universal. Every interaction entangles what takes part in it. We fail to see it because the correlations disperse into the environment fast enough that any local system looks classical. This is decoherence. A cup of coffee is entangled with the air, the light and the hand that holds it, beyond any possibility of detection.

Three interpretations remain, each at a price. Copenhagen: indeterminacy is fundamental. Bohm: the world is determinate, but one wave function for the entire universe guides every particle at once, and separate objects are an approximation. Many-worlds: every outcome occurs, and the uncertainty concerns which branch one is in.

Copenhagen gives the Pyrrhonist an unexpected result. He suspended judgment because he could not know what things are. Under Copenhagen, before measurement there is often nothing definite to know. Bohm gives another: the boundaries between objects are appearances as well.

From total non-belief, this reads as agreement. The universe holds no definite position until it is forced to take one.
