_The hub for everything in Publications: one argument, six rooms, and a map of every essay._

I write about the state, religion, language models, markets, video games and sprinting. The subjects look separate. Read together, they make one argument.

There is nothing behind the words. No God keeps accounts, no History is headed anywhere, no natural law holds whether or not anyone enforces it. Take the guarantor away and what's left in any room is people, what they want, and what each of them can do to the others. That remainder is leverage.

Every organized system answers the same way. The state takes a census, the firm makes a forecast, the platform builds a model of its user, the lab trains toward the center of the distribution, the board removes variance. Whatever can be counted gets managed, and whatever can't is priced at zero. The person is the part that can't be counted, and freedom is the name for the gap between what is determined about you and what is known.

Inside that gap you live with a want you didn't choose. You can resent it or own it. If you own it, you spend your years carving a shape, mostly by exclusion, and the shape is the only place your life ever existed. Nobody keeps it afterwards, and you cut with full intensity anyway.

## Six rooms

**Power.** Every system built to free people becomes the next cage. Freedom is earned through leverage, morality is the ledger the few hand the many, and the century's politics runs between two measures of freedom: what you can do, and what can't be known about you. The liberator's only exit is to work toward his own unnecessity. → [Every Cage Was a Door](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f28260814b9e58db9fb4d5dec4)

**Belief.** Certainty about the destination is the tell of theology, whether the destination is heaven, History or the Singularity. People halt their thinking where it starts to cost them. Clear the head, hold maps as maps, and treat the convergence of traditions as evidence without letting anyone own the object. → [The Tell](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f2826081f89df9ecf54a977efc)

**The machine.** A language model is a photocopy of the map, pulled toward the median and answerable to whoever holds its loop. Grant it the whole signal. What it lacks is a vessel with something at stake. → [The Map That Doesn't Walk](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f2826081fb9d1ecf92bfbb965b)

**The self.** You didn't choose the chooser. The lamp cannot light itself, and it burns. Own the want, guard the gap that makes you unpredictable, and keep the door out of the cycle open without pretending you're ready to walk through it. → [The Lamp](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f282608152abe6d9fe225b3723)

**Markets.** Money is stored freedom and always raises the question of what for. Greed at scale runs on a schedule, leverage writes down where the crowd will be forced to sell, and the edge is to stand there with capital while keeping the crowd in your own head off the trigger. → [The Counterparty](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f2826081eebd4ef576e99147f8)

**The life.** Meaning is a search that never arrives. Most of everything is filler, flow is mostly subtraction, and a life gets its final shape from repeated completion and the things it refuses. → [The Final Shape](https://app.notion.com/p/422143f28260819791dde92ef3b9475a)

## The tensions

These run across the rooms, and I've chosen to leave them standing. Each one is an essay I haven't written yet.

- **Optionality against shape.** Never anchor, keep every exit open, rotate with the regime. And: perpetual optionality was defeat, choose the shape and stay inside it. Trading rewards the first. A life may need the second.
- **Clearing against carving.** A cleared mind holds nothing fixed. A carved life closes into one form. Maybe one governs belief and the other conduct.
- **Leverage against decency.** The strong do what they can, and the means still justify the end. The realist and the humanist share one byline, and neither has absorbed the other.
- **No destination against a culminating freedom.** I call certainty about History's direction the tell, and I've also written that ideals carry history toward a culminating freedom. The second survives only as a bet.
- **What you can do against what can't be known.** A perfect government would pass the first test and fail the second. I haven't decided which one I would give up.
- **Fate against will.** The boxes were filled before you walked in, and reality bends to the will of men. One is a description from outside and the other is how the burning feels from inside.
